Vetting Process

How We Choose Our Partners

Less than 12% of breeder applicants make it through our vetting process. Here's exactly what that looks like.

01
Application Review

Breeders submit proof of TICA or CFA registration, health testing records, and facility photos. Our team reviews every document manually — no automated screening.

02
On-Site Audit

Dr. Slockish Alessandra, our Head of Breeder Relations, personally visits every facility. Our 40-point checklist covers living space, enrichment, socialisation, nutrition, and veterinary care standards.

03
Genetic Verification

All breeding pairs must have documented genetic health screens for breed-specific conditions. We cross-reference results with an independent veterinary pathologist before approving any litter.

04
Annual Re-Audit

Approval is never permanent. Every partner receives an unannounced re-audit each year. Any breeder who falls below standard is immediately suspended pending remediation — or removed from the network entirely.

The Standard

Our 40-Point Welfare Checklist

Every criterion a breeder must meet — and maintain — to carry the HelloKitty seal of approval.

Minimum 4m² indoor space per adult cat

Enforced regardless of breed size. Overcrowding is grounds for immediate suspension.

Natural light access every day

Cats must have access to a window or outdoor-adjacent area with sunlight for a minimum of 4 hours daily.

Bi-annual HCM cardiac screening

Performed by a board-certified veterinary cardiologist, not a general practitioner.

Breed-specific genetic panel annually

PKD for Persians, PRA for Bengals, SMA for Maine Coons — every breeding pair, every year.

Full FVRCP vaccination series

Administered by a licensed vet, not the breeder. Records must accompany every kitten.

Daily kitten handling from week two

Kittens must be handled by multiple people daily to ensure healthy socialisation before placement.

Deworming & flea prevention protocol

All kittens treated at 2, 4, 6, and 8 weeks. Certification required at point of sale.

TICA or CFA registration on all litters

Pedigree documentation must be verifiable by the HelloKitty team independently before any listing goes live.
