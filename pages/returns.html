Our returns policy is designed with both your rights and the welfare of our kittens in mind. Read through everything before you purchase.

A Note on Kitten Welfare

Unlike a product, a kitten is a living being who forms bonds and experiences stress from rehoming. HelloKitty does not accept "change of mind" returns. Instead, we invest deeply in helping you choose the right breed, understand care requirements, and prepare your home — so returns are rarely needed. If exceptional circumstances arise, our welfare team will always work with you to find the most compassionate solution.

Policy Overview

Kittens vs. Accessories

Kittens

Returns of live kittens are not accepted for change of mind. However, the following situations are handled compassionately:

Health Guarantee Claim: If a covered condition is diagnosed within 30 days, a replacement kitten or refund is offered per our Health Guarantee terms.
Emergency Rehoming: If you genuinely cannot keep your kitten, contact our welfare team. We maintain a trusted rehoming network and will facilitate a safe placement at no charge to you.
HelloKitty-Initiated Cancellation: If we cancel your order for any reason, you receive a 100% refund, no questions asked.
Accessories & Supplies

Accessories can be returned within 14 days of delivery if they meet the following conditions:

Item is unused, undamaged, and in original packaging
Return is initiated by emailing returns@hellokitty.store within the 14-day window
Food, treats, supplements, and opened hygiene products cannot be returned for safety reasons
Defective or damaged items are covered regardless of time, with photographic evidence
