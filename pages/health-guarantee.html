Claims Process

How to Make a Claim

We keep it simple. No call centres, no lengthy forms — just quick, compassionate support.

1
Contact Our Vet Hotline Immediately

Call or WhatsApp our 24/7 vet line (+1 503 555 0148) as soon as you notice a concern. Our team will assess symptoms and guide your next steps. Don't wait — early action is always better for your kitten.

2
Visit a Licensed Veterinarian

Take your kitten to a licensed vet for a formal diagnosis. Ask for a written report detailing the condition, diagnosis method, and their professional opinion on cause. We cover any breed-registered vet worldwide.

3
Submit Your Claim Online

Email claims@hellokitty.store with your order number, the vet's written report, and itemised receipts. Our team reviews all claims within 5 business days.

4
Receive Your Resolution

Once approved, vet bill reimbursements are processed via bank transfer within 3 business days. If a replacement kitten is chosen, we'll work with you to select one that's a perfect match for your home.
