Documentation

What's Included in Every Kitten Shipment

Every international and domestic kitten delivery comes with a complete documentation package prepared by our team.

USDA Health Certificate

Issued by a USDA-accredited veterinarian within 10 days of travel, as required for all US exports. Endorsed by the USDA APHIS office.

Vaccination Records

Full FVRCP series vaccination history, dated and signed by our licensed vet. Continued schedule included for your local vet.

Microchip Certificate

ISO 11784/11785-compliant 15-digit microchip number and pre-completed transfer of ownership form for your country's registry.

Pedigree Papers

Official CFA or TICA registration papers verifying your kitten's lineage, breed standard compliance, and breeder accreditation.

Health Guarantee Document

Signed 30-day congenital health guarantee and 2-year hereditary warranty. Includes claim procedures and our vet hotline number.

Welcome Kit

Breed care guide, feeding schedule, recommended vet visit timeline, toy, familiar-scent blanket, and 24/7 vet hotline card.

Our Commitment

The HelloKitty Shipping Promise

Climate-Controlled Transit

Every transport vehicle and airline cargo hold used by HelloKitty maintains 68–72°F throughout the journey. Your kitten's comfort is non-negotiable.

Live GPS Tracking

You receive a live tracking link the morning of delivery. Check your kitten's location in real time from origin to your front door.

Dedicated Pet Handler

Every domestic delivery is accompanied by a trained HelloKitty pet handler. Your kitten is never left unattended at any point during transit.

No Overnight Layovers

We plan routes to ensure your kitten is never left in a cargo facility overnight. If a delay occurs, our team arranges immediate foster care at our expense.

Full Insurance Coverage

All kitten shipments are insured for full purchase value during transit. In the extremely unlikely event of loss or harm, you are fully protected.

Worldwide Guarantee

Our welfare promise applies wherever you are. Whether you're in Portland, Paris, or Perth — your kitten is protected by our 30-day health guarantee.
