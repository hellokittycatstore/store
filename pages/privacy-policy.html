01

Overview

HelloKitty Worldwide ("HelloKitty", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website hellokitty.store and all associated services. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our site, create an account, purchase a kitten or accessory, or contact our team.

We are committed to data minimisation — we collect only what we genuinely need to run our service well, and we never sell your personal data to third parties. Period.

The short version: we use your data to fulfil your order, keep you informed about it, and improve our platform. We never sell it, never share it with advertisers, and you can request deletion at any time.

This policy applies to all users worldwide. Where applicable, we comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023).

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies — small text files placed on your device — to make our site work correctly and to understand how it is used. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration Can be disabled?
Essential Cart contents, session authentication, security tokens Session / up to 30 days No — site will not function correctly without them
Functional Remembered preferences, wishlist, currency/language settings Up to 12 months Yes — via your browser settings
Analytics Anonymised page views, session duration, referral source Up to 24 months Yes — via cookie consent settings
Live Chat (Smartsupp) Chat session continuity and support history Up to 6 months Yes — via cookie consent settings

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers also let you block cookies entirely or receive a prompt before each one is set. Note that disabling functional cookies may affect features like your wishlist and saved preferences.

07

How We Protect Your Data

We take the security of your information seriously and apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

Encryption in Transit

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Our site is served exclusively over HTTPS.

Encryption at Rest

Personal data stored in our databases is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Backups are encrypted using the same standard.

PCI-DSS Compliant Payments

We never store your full card number. All payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors. We only see a tokenised reference and the last 4 digits.

Access Controls

Access to personal data is restricted to team members who need it to do their job. All access is logged, audited, and protected by multi-factor authentication.

Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to you, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay, as required by law.

No system is 100% secure. If you discover a potential security vulnerability on our platform, please report it responsibly to security@hellokitty.store before disclosing it publicly. We take all such reports seriously.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the following rights over your personal data. We honour all of them, regardless of jurisdiction.

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you. We will provide it in a machine-readable format within 30 days.

Right to Rectification

Ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data. You can also update most information directly in your account settings.

Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

Request deletion of your personal data. We will comply unless we are required to retain it for a legal obligation (such as 7-year financial records).

Right to Restrict Processing

Ask us to pause processing your data — for example, while you contest its accuracy or object to how we use it — without deleting it.

Right to Data Portability

Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON or CSV) to transfer to another service provider.

Right to Object

Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including direct marketing. We will stop processing immediately upon a valid objection to marketing.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on your consent (e.g. marketing emails), you can withdraw that consent at any time. There is an unsubscribe link at the bottom of every marketing email we send.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@hellokitty.store with your full name and the email address associated with your account. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing a sensitive request.

California residents (CCPA): You have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale of personal information. HelloKitty does not sell personal information. To make a CCPA request, email us at privacy@hellokitty.store or call +1 (503) 555-0192.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@hellokitty.store and we will delete it promptly.

If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 16, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our systems.

International Data Transfers

HelloKitty operates globally. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than the one you live in — including the United States, where our primary infrastructure is hosted.

When we transfer data outside your home country, we rely on one or more of the following safeguards:

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

For transfers from the EU/EEA to countries without an adequacy decision, we use the European Commission's approved Standard Contractual Clauses.

Adequacy Decisions

Where the destination country has received an adequacy decision from the relevant data protection authority, we rely on that finding.

Data Processing Agreements

All third-party processors — regardless of location — are bound by data processing agreements requiring appropriate security standards and limiting their use of your data.

You can request details of the specific safeguards in place for any particular transfer by contacting our privacy team.
