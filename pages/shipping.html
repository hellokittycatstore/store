Documentation

What's Included in Every Kitten Shipment

Every international and domestic kitten delivery comes with a complete documentation package prepared by our team.

USDA Health CertificateIssued by a USDA-accredited veterinarian within 10 days of travel, as required for all US exports. Endorsed by the USDA APHIS office.
Vaccination RecordsFull FVRCP series vaccination history, dated and signed by our licensed vet. Continued schedule included for your local vet.
Microchip CertificateISO 11784/11785-compliant 15-digit microchip number and pre-completed transfer of ownership form for your country's registry.
Pedigree PapersOfficial CFA or TICA registration papers verifying your kitten's lineage, breed standard compliance, and breeder accreditation.
Health Guarantee DocumentSigned 30-day congenital health guarantee and 2-year hereditary warranty. Includes claim procedures and our vet hotline number.
Welcome KitBreed care guide, feeding schedule, recommended vet visit timeline, toy, familiar-scent blanket, and 24/7 vet hotline card.
