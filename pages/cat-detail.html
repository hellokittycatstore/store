Feeding

Feed high-quality, breed-appropriate kitten food 3–4 times daily until 6 months, then twice daily. Always provide fresh water.

Grooming

Brush regularly based on coat type. Schedule vet check-ups every 6 months. Keep nails trimmed and ears clean.

Exercise

Provide interactive toys, cat trees, and play sessions daily. Mental stimulation is as important as physical activity.

Health

Keep vaccinations current, apply regular flea and worm treatment, and have your vet monitor breed-specific health conditions.

Preparation

Your kitten receives a final health exam, vaccination booster if due, full grooming, and a HelloKitty welcome kit including food, blanket, and care guide.

Delivery

All deliveries use climate-controlled vehicles with a dedicated pet handler. Live GPS tracking is shared with you the morning of delivery. Nationwide coverage — door to door.

Health Guarantee

All kittens come with a 30-day health guarantee and 2-year pedigree warranty. If any hereditary condition is found, we cover vet bills or offer a full replacement.
