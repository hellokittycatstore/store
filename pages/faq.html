Buying a Kitten
Add your chosen kitten to the cart and proceed to checkout. A 30% deposit (paid via bank transfer or card) officially reserves your kitten and removes it from sale. The balance is due before delivery. You'll receive an order confirmation email within minutes of payment.

Delivery
After payment confirmation, we coordinate with the breeder for a final health check and grooming. Delivery typically takes 3–7 days. On the day, you receive a live GPS tracking link and a dedicated pet handler stays with your kitten throughout the journey. Your kitten arrives with a welcome kit (health records, food sample, care guide).
We deliver to all 36 Nigerian states. For locations within Lagos, delivery is typically same-day or next-day. Nationwide deliveries take 2–5 business days and use climate-controlled, purpose-built pet transport vehicles. International delivery to select West African countries is available on request.
Delivery within Lagos Island and Mainland is complimentary on all kitten purchases. Nationwide delivery costs are calculated at checkout based on your state. We never add surprise fees — the price shown at checkout is the final price.
Yes. Collection is available from our Lekki office (12 Admiralty Way) by appointment, or directly from the breeder's facility if you prefer. Just let us know your preference at checkout and we'll coordinate.
Health & Guarantee
Every HelloKitty kitten comes with a 30-day congenital health guarantee. If your kitten is diagnosed with an inherited or congenital condition by a licensed vet within 30 days, we cover vet bills up to the purchase price or offer a full replacement kitten of equal value. A 2-year hereditary condition warranty is also included for breed-specific diseases (e.g., PKD in Persians, HCM in Maine Coons).
Yes. All kittens receive their core vaccinations (FVRCP series) appropriate for their age before leaving the breeder. Vaccination records are included in the welcome kit. We also provide a recommended vaccination schedule for the first year so your vet can continue seamlessly.
Most kittens are microchipped — this is indicated clearly on each listing with a "Microchipped" badge. For kittens not yet microchipped, we offer a complimentary microchipping service during delivery. All microchip registrations are transferred to the new owner.
Contact our 24/7 vet hotline immediately. Our team will assess the symptoms and advise you. If the illness is linked to a condition pre-existing before delivery, our health guarantee applies. We will never leave a HelloKitty family without support — this is a core promise.
Payments
We accept bank transfers to our HelloKitty escrow account (GTBank & Zenith), all major debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), and Flutterwave. A 30% deposit reserves your kitten; the balance is due before delivery.
All card payments are processed through Flutterwave, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We never store your card details. Bank transfers go to a verified HelloKitty escrow account — we provide the account number only to confirmed buyers and it will always match our registered business name.
We offer a flexible payment plan on select kittens priced above ₦500,000 (or $500). A 30% deposit holds the kitten for up to 30 days, with the balance payable before delivery. Contact us to discuss an instalment plan if needed.
If you cancel before the kitten is dispatched, we refund 70% of the deposit (30% is non-refundable to cover breeder preparation costs). If HelloKitty cancels for any reason, you receive a full 100% refund. After delivery, refunds are governed by our health guarantee and returns policy — see the Returns page for details.

Accessories
Accessories are dispatched from our Lagos warehouse. Orders placed before 2pm are shipped same day and typically arrive within 1–3 business days in Lagos, and 3–7 days nationwide.
Accessories can be returned within 14 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Food, treats, and opened hygiene items are non-returnable for safety reasons. See our full Returns Policy for the process.
Every food and supplement on HelloKitty has been reviewed by our in-house veterinary team. We only stock grain-free, high-protein formulas with clearly listed ingredients, and we never carry products with artificial preservatives or harmful additives.
