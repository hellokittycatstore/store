Our Mission

Every Kitten Deserves a Loving Home

HelloKitty was founded in Lagos in 2022 by a team of veterinarians, breeders, and cat enthusiasts who saw a gap in the market: too many families were unknowingly buying sick or misrepresented kittens from unregulated sellers.

We built a platform where every kitten listed is health-certified, pedigree-verified, and backed by a genuine breeder relationship. Our job isn't just to sell cats — it's to ensure a lifetime of love between you and your companion.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

01

Health First

Every kitten undergoes a full veterinary examination before listing. Vaccinations, deworming, and microchipping are standard — never optional.

02

Breeder Trust

We only partner with breeders who meet our 40-point welfare standard. Every partnership is annually reviewed and every litter is inspected.

03

Lifetime Support

Our relationship doesn't end at delivery. Every HelloKitty family gets access to our vet hotline, community forums, and nutrition advisors — for life.

04

Ethical Breeding

We actively campaign against kitten mills and irresponsible breeding. Every purchase supports breeders who genuinely love their animals.

05

Radical Transparency

Full health history, genetic tests, and lineage documentation for every kitten. No hidden surprises — ever.

06

Safe Delivery

Climate-controlled vehicles, GPS tracking, and a dedicated pet handler on every delivery. Your kitten arrives calm and comfortable.

Standards

Our Breeder Standards

Every partner breeder is held to a comprehensive 40-point welfare standard, audited annually.

Living Conditions

All cats must have adequate indoor space, enrichment, natural light, and be free from overcrowding. Minimum space requirements enforced.

Medical Protocols

Mandatory vaccination schedules, deworming, and flea treatment. Annual blood panels for breeding cats. Full vet records available on request.

Genetic Testing

All breeding pairs screened for breed-specific hereditary conditions. PKD for Persians, HCM for Maine Coons, and more.

Socialisation

Kittens must be handled daily from birth and exposed to domestic sounds, children, and other pets before placement.

Documentation

Full pedigree papers, health certificates, vaccination records, and microchip registration must accompany every kitten.

Annual Audits

Every breeder partnership is reviewed annually with unannounced visits. Non-compliance results in immediate suspension.
