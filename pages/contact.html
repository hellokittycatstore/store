Once your order is confirmed and payment received, we coordinate with the breeder to prepare your kitten (final health check, grooming, welcome kit). Delivery typically takes 3–7 days depending on location. You'll receive live GPS tracking the morning of delivery, and a dedicated pet handler accompanies your kitten the entire way.
Every HelloKitty kitten comes with a 30-day health guarantee. If your kitten is diagnosed with a congenital condition within 30 days, we cover vet bills up to the purchase price or offer a full replacement. A 2-year pedigree warranty is also included for hereditary conditions specific to the breed.

We accept bank transfers to our HelloKitty escrow account, all major credit/debit cards, and Flutterwave. A 30% deposit secures a kitten, with the balance due before delivery. We never release kitten details to strangers without a confirmed deposit.
We currently deliver nationwide across all 36 Nigerian states. International delivery is available to select West African countries — please contact us directly for a quote and to confirm requirements including import documentation.
