Frequently Asked Questions

We ship worldwide from our Portland, Oregon headquarters. Once your order is confirmed, we coordinate USDA-approved health certificates, CITES permits (where required), and IATA-compliant airline-approved carriers. A dedicated pet travel coordinator manages your kitten's journey door-to-door. Most international deliveries take 3–10 business days.
Every HelloKitty kitten comes with a 30-day congenital health guarantee. If your kitten is diagnosed with a congenital condition within 30 days, we cover vet bills up to the purchase price or offer a full replacement. A 2-year warranty is also included for breed-specific hereditary conditions.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx), bank wire transfer, Stripe, and PayPal. A 30% deposit reserves your kitten; the balance is due before dispatch. International buyers may pay in USD via wire or Stripe.
HelloKitty ships to 60+ countries worldwide. We handle all export documentation from the US side. Import permits, quarantine requirements, and any applicable import duties are the buyer's responsibility — we provide a full guide for your country when you place an order.
