Our Process

How We Hire

We respect your time. Our process is four steps, takes under three weeks, and will never include a surprise task you weren't told about in advance.

1
Application Review

Every application is read by a human — not filtered by keyword. You'll hear back within 5 business days, either way.

2
Intro Call

30 minutes with our hiring lead. We want to understand who you are, what you care about, and whether HelloKitty is actually a good fit for you — not just the reverse.

3
Skill Assessment

A short, paid task relevant to the role — maximum 2 hours. We pay $75 for every completed assessment, regardless of outcome.

4
Team Interview & Offer

Meet two or three team members in a single 60-minute conversation. If it's a match, we move to offer within 48 hours.
